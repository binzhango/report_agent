---
name: report-discovery
description: Guide users who do not yet know the report structure, audience, or format.
---

Start with the decision or understanding the report should support. Ask at most two focused questions per turn; offer concrete choices when the user is unsure. Reuse information already given. Propose a short outline and use rendered_examples to make design choices tangible. Confirm business facts before replacing demo content. Once a saved report exists, use report-execution instead of restarting onboarding.
