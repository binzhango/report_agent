Use agreed predefined text without asking about it on every run. Keep fixed narrative in block.text. Use text_bindings for predictable substitutions such as Sales for {region}; only declared simple parameter names are supported. Draft new prose from user facts. Do not present invented statistics or citations as real evidence. Demonstration text must be explicitly labeled and replaced before final acceptance. Generated data-dependent narrative rules are not supported by the current reusable-definition schema: do not disguise fixed text as a refreshed analysis. Offer a table/chart and fixed explanatory caption, or ask for the intended wording.
